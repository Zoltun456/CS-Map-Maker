Permafrost

Working name de_permafrost. A remote Arctic research station. The outside is a frozen supply yard behind a perimeter fence. The inside is a sealed weapons research wing: clean labs, containment doors and decontamination airlocks. The agents and the program are fictional, so the signage can use made-up codenames like Compound K-7.

Ts come in across the ice. CTs are station security and spawn inside. Site A is the outdoor cargo pad where sealed containers wait for airlift. Site B is the containment lab at the heart of the building.

It uses the sample's routes, so the timings and balance carry over. Blue-grey floor is indoors. In the simulator it comes out close to even.

Zones and callouts

Callout Where Set dressing Play note
Snowcat drop T spawn, outside Tracked snow vehicles, flare stands, fuel drums, tyre ruts in the snow. Vehicles block any view toward the building.
Fence A long, outside Chain-link perimeter fence, floodlight towers, snowdrifts. Drifts are waist-high cover. The long doors become a gate in the fence.
Cargo pad Site A, outside Helipad markings, sealed containers with hazard stencils, a crane or snowcat. Containers are the site's cover. Paint a huge A on the pad.
Boiler house A short, inside Boilers, steam pipes (static, no vapour), metal stairs. The only indoor route on the T side. Grated floors are loud.
Courtyard Mid, outside Radio mast, satellite dish, frozen generator. Main contact point. The dish is good cover for a mid fight.
Main corridor Mid to CT, inside Observation windows into the labs, notice boards, ID turnstiles. Keep windows as solid glass so they don't add sightlines.
Security lobby CT spawn, inside Reception desk, monitors, weapons lockers. Buy zone.
Containment Site B, inside Glass fume hoods, sample freezers, steel benches, a vault-style containment door. Benches are the cover. The vault door frames the CT entry.
Decon Mid to B, inside Double doors, yellow and black floor stripes, shower heads, PPE lockers. Narrow by design. Home of the blast-door trap.
Service tunnels B tunnels, inside Pipe runs, heating ducts, hazard tape, caged lamps. Long and dim. Home of the alarm trap.

Look and materials

Outside

Packed snow, ice-blue shadow, dark spruce and concrete, safety orange on anything people touch.

Inside

White tile, pale institutional green, stainless steel, hazard yellow, and red only on alarms and lockdown lights.

Stock content to search for

  • Office has snowy exteriors, snow-covered props and office interiors for the lobby and corridor.
  • Nuke has industrial interiors, lockers, catwalks, hazard signs and pipework for the tunnels, decon and boiler house.
  • Vertigo and Train add scaffolding, cranes, fences and heavy industrial pieces for the cargo pad.
  • In the Asset Browser, try the words snow, hazard, locker, pipe, lab, fence and crate. Exact asset names change, so search rather than relying on a list.

Make these yourself

  • Decals: the station logo, hazard stencils, room numbers and the big A and B. A decal is one image with transparency, so it's the easiest custom asset to make.
  • Snow blend: a material that blends snow over concrete or asphalt, so paths look trodden and edges look drifted. Poly Haven and ambientCG both have CC0 snow sets.
  • Lab signage: fictional compound codes, evacuation maps and warning posters. They tell the story without any real chemistry.

Lighting and doorways

  • Outside: overcast, low sun, soft shadows, cold blue sky light. Light snowfall is fine for mood, but keep it thin and never across a long sightline.
  • Inside: cool fluorescent panels in the labs and corridors, warmer sodium lamps in the tunnels and boiler house.
  • Doorways need care. The camera adjusts its exposure as you move between bright snow and a dim interior. Whoever holds the doorway from inside gets a moment where the attacker sees badly. Keep the first few metres inside bright, use a lit vestibule at each entrance, and limit how far auto-exposure can swing in a post-processing volume.
  • Snow glare: pure white snow behind a player at eye level makes them a dark silhouette, which is fair to both teams. Glare reflections are not, so keep snow matte.

Sound

  • Wind outside, ventilation hum inside, quiet electrical buzz in the labs. Set these with soundscapes so they change as you walk through a door.
  • Footsteps are information in CS. Give snow, metal grating, lab tile and concrete different surface sounds so players can hear where someone is. Grating in the boiler house and decon makes rushing those routes loud on purpose.

Traps

You're right to be wary. Traps in CS tend to go wrong in a few predictable ways, so each idea below is built to avoid them. None of them use gas, smoke or vapour.

How traps get abused

Problem Safeguard
Spamming the button Once per round. The button locks after use and resets at round start.
Triggering it by shooting from across the map Press-to-use only, at close range, never on damage.
Killing or blocking your own team No damage traps, or damage filtered to one team.
Stalling the clock CTs win on time, so any door that delays Ts is strong. Keep closures short (about 8 seconds) and let either team use them.
Locking the bomb or the defuse No trap can touch a bombsite, block the bomb, or stop a defuse.
Traps at round start Disabled for the first 20 seconds, so nobody uses them on a rush or at spawn.
Surprise deaths Every trap gives a 2 second warning with a klaxon and flashing lights before it acts.
A trap state carrying into the next round Everything resets at round start. Test this, because it's the most common bug.

Ideas

Trap What it does Risk Verdict
Decon blast door A wall panel in decon seals the airlock for 8 seconds after a 2 second klaxon, then it reopens. Either team can press it, once per round. It cuts a rotation or splits an attack, and the person pressing it is exposed while they do it. Medium Build first
Tunnel alarm A laser tripwire in the service tunnels. When a T crosses it, red lights and an alarm play for 5 seconds, telling CTs someone is coming. Ts can shoot the emitter to disarm it for the round. Low Build first
Breakable ice window A frosted window on the fence route that can be shot out, opening a new angle onto the cargo pad. Repaired each round. Low Good extra
Electrified grate A panel in the boiler house powers a floor grate for 3 seconds, doing light damage with sparks as a warning. Damage only to the other team. High Only after testing
Power cut, floodlight blind Turning off lights or blinding a choke point. High Skip

Playtest Permafrost without traps first, then publish a second version, de_permafrost_traps, so you can compare. The simulator doesn't model traps, so those tests have to be with friends.

Building the traps

Each trap is a few logic entities wired together with outputs. These entities come from CS:GO and most work the same in CS2 Hammer, but check names and options on the VDC wiki as you go.

Decon blast door

  • The door is a mesh tied to func_door. Set it to stop or reverse when blocked and do no crush damage, so nobody dies under it.
  • The panel is a func_button set to press-to-use only, not damage-activated, and to stay pressed so it can't be used again.
  • Pressing it fires a logic_relay: start the klaxon and lights, close the door after 2 seconds, open it again 8 seconds later.
  • For the 20 second delay, start the button locked and unlock it with a timed output when the round begins.

Tunnel alarm

  • A thin trigger_multiple across the tunnel with a filter_activator_team set to Terrorists.
  • It fires a relay that turns the red lights on, plays the alarm, and turns them off after 5 seconds.
  • The emitter is a small func_breakable. When it breaks, it disables the trigger.
